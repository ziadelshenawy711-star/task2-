1. Introduction

E-Commerce means buying and selling products or services through the internet. Customers can shop online using websites instead of visiting physical stores.

2. Main Topic

How Does an Online Store Work?

An online store displays products and their prices. Customers choose products, add them to the shopping cart, enter their address, and confirm their orders. The store then prepares and delivers the order.

Main Components

  • Product Catalog: Displays products and prices.
  • Shopping Cart: Stores selected products.
  • Payment System: Allows customers to pay.
  • Database: Stores customer and product information.
  • Delivery: Sends products to customers.

How Customers Place an Order

  1. Visit the online store.
  2. Select a product.
  3. Add it to the shopping cart.
  4. Enter the delivery address.
  5. Choose a payment method.
  6. Confirm the order.
  7. Receive the order.
